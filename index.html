Empty consulting rooms, booked by the hour

How we built a healthcare marketplace from the first wireframe to the Web Summit Lisbon stage.

An “Airbnb” for medical offices.

01Product 02Process 03Blueprint 04Heatmap 05Prototype
DesktopRoom page and weekly calendar MobileRooms from R$ 30/h
Role
Co-founder & CTO
Timeline
Mar 2023 – Dec 2024
Team
Outsourced dev squad
Where
Natal, Brazil

Problem

Early-career health professionals need a place to see patients, but their own office means fixed rent, furniture and reception. Meanwhile clinics have rooms sitting empty for hours every week.

Solution

A marketplace with search by neighbourhood, a page for each room with photos and amenities, an hourly calendar and online booking. Clinics earn from idle hours; professionals pay only for the time they use.

My part

Discovery and backlog prioritisation, search and booking flows, managing the development squad, the UX/UI redesign and launch. I also created the brand's social media.
